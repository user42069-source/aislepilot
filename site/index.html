A clear view of your trip

  1. Find real places.

    Confirm a geocoded address. OpenStreetMap supplies nearby stores, coordinates and any available street addresses.

  2. Compare the drive.

    OSRM supplies road travel times. The planner finds the shortest sequence that covers your categories within a shortlist of up to three nearby stores per category.

  3. Check the evidence.

    A connected Chromium browser looks for public product offers. It respects robots.txt and access blocks. Online availability is different from stock at your selected branch.

  4. Build an honest budget.

    Matching online offers can supply candidate estimates. Unknowns stay unknown; add your own unit budgets. Specific quantities such as pounds and dozens need a budget in that unit.

Public data services are best-effort and maps can be incomplete. Opening hours are shown as source data; the route does not filter for stores currently open.
